# Glossary — BuyPool

* Allocation: proportional distribution of available units across participations, with remainders assigned by largest remainder.
* Assignment: confirmed share of units resulting from allocation for a successful campaign.
* Balance: remaining payment after the deposit, computed at the final tier price plus commission and delivery cost.
* Broadcast: in-app notification sent to all active customers when a campaign is published.
* Campaign: group purchase with a product, MOQ, current quantity, deadline, status, prices, and pickup point.
* Commission: platform fee percentage applied to the goods amount.
* Delivery: fulfillment record linked to one participation, either pickup or home delivery.
* Deposit: advance payment through Stripe that creates a participation.
* Home delivery: shipment to the customer address at a fixed delivery cost.
* MOQ: minimum order quantity required for a campaign to succeed.
* Notification: in-app message about order, payment, shipment, publication, or system events.
* Participation: one customer join in a campaign with quantity, deposit, balance, and status.
* Pickup: free collection at a pickup point proven by a single-use QR code.
* Price tier: quantity threshold above which a lower unit price applies.
* Product: catalog item supplied by a supplier, distinct from a proposal.
* Proposal: suggested product idea that can be voted on and published as a campaign.
* QR code: single-use pickup token linked to one participation.
* Refund: Stripe reimbursement of a deposit after withdrawal or failed closure.
* Supplier: company account that provides products and fulfills supplier orders.
* Supplier order: order sent to a supplier for one campaign, advanced through fixed fulfillment steps.
* Suspension: restriction where a user keeps read access but most write actions are forbidden.
* Vote: single favorable or opposing choice by one user on one proposal.
